About Azzets

Our Story

Azzets is one of the leading concern in Real Estate business more than a decade. We are a Chennai based company dealing with the land promotion and selling activities. We sell and resell plots, lands and properties with gated community and several amenities facilitated at prominent and fast developing areas in Chennai. Many of the investors, customers and actual users have satisfied their dreams of getting their homes with quality construction and timely possession. For us, “Your dream is our Mission.”

Vision

To be the market leader in Gated Community and Residential Plot Development Projects throughout India by providing unrivalled customer value.

Mission

Our commitment is to develop Gated Community and Residential Plot Development Projects which correlate to international standards and provide you with the best of all.
